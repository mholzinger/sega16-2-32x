# Compact scaled-pose experiment

Mike reports full play works after rdplay01 was restored. Preserve that accepted
baseline. The raw scaled atlas c3e0371f observed a fixed-scene plot saving but no
whole-run hardware gain and stays withheld. This experiment tests whether its
storage tax can be reduced while retaining its supported poses.

## Component and ownership

Sourcef794595d. R60ZOOMPACK=1 implies R60ZOOMBAKE and requires R60SPRPREP. Default OFF. The baker
retains the **raw-budget selection**, then packs two raw pens per byte and pads
each row payload to a longword. ZP1 retains ZB1 frame/index/negative pixel-end
headers. Same42keys,49140 ->28280bytes:20860bytes returned to unscaled art.
Remaining unscaled poses are re-ranked by the existing allocator; link guards
and mapper rules are unchanged. No extra poses silently fill the savings.

The generic C r60_zoom_unpack.h preflights input extents and both buffer limits,
then clears the existing prepared-sprite cache's valid flag before writing its
4KiB scratch. It produces the existing raw packet, including its frame header.
The established assembly copy loop remains unchanged. Missing, clipped or
oversized poses fall back to the scaler; refusals leave the old cache untouched.
Clipping and height are checked before expansion, so rejected offscreen work does
not evict the cache or pay decode cost. No new persistent RAM arena is allocated.
Unpacking adds per-record work; a smaller image alone does not prove a speed win.

## Hardware result: keep experimental

Fixed native7544 drawing cost: original control1056scanline units, raw atlas768,
compact atlas888 (peak896); seven validated screenshots per launch. Compact
recovers storage but adds decoding work: about16% less plot time than the original
in this fixture, versus about27% for raw. Compact total pass median5.649VInts,
raw5.679, original controls6.473/6.443. These are separate launches, one compact
launch, and are not a three-launch promotion gate. The fixed fixture repeatedly
unpacks planes on the master, freezes gameplay, uses full clearing/quiet audio;
its bus load is artificial and cannot establish normal game FPS.

Live compact MiSTer capture:25valid samples/zero rejections, native progress/VInt
