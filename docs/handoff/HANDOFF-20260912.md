## THE BAR, AND WHY IT IS A CLIFF

60 Hz = **100% single-vint frames**. Currently 20%.

**Ships are vint-quantised, so the bar is a THRESHOLD at 1.00 vint, not
a gradient** (168). A generation finishing at 1.2 vints still costs 2 and
flips at 30 Hz. Nothing is paid until the wall crosses below 1.00, then
15% -> 98% arrives at once. **Every compose diet before this session was
ranked against a gradient that does not exist**, which is why eight days
of work moved numbers and never moved the picture.

**60 Hz IS reachable and the pipeline was never the constraint.**
Ablating the maps drain and the sprite compose: **58.3 fps, 98%
single-vint** (168). The transport, flip, packet path and 68K handler all
fit inside one vblank with room spare.

## TRAPS THIS SESSION PAID FOR

  1. **Two builds sampled at the same FRAME NUMBER are not at the same
     point in the GAME.** Five of my comparisons broke on this (185 lists
     all five). Any rate with the game in its numerator must be aligned
     on the game's own timeline first, which is what
     `attract_parity.py` does and `HANDOFF-SESSION6` explains.
  2. **Counter collisions, three times.** `DIAG[39]/[50]/[53]` are each
     written by three subsystems including the DREQ path (152). The
     0x28F80-0x29000 scratch map is now written into m_main.c above the

  3. **The only trustworthy game-frame counter is `PASSCOUNT=1`** (185).
     The scene timer is per-scene and scene-dependent in rate AND
     direction; the miss counter is cleared by the game itself at 0x930;
     a counter in the shared wait counts attract's 120- and 240-frame
     delays as frames.
