You still get the 8.5× smaller packet, ~8× tiles per window, and the SH-2 out of the tile path entirely. What you lose is that the 68K now does 16 reads + 16 writes per tile by CPU instead of the VDP's DMA engine doing it.

## The mechanisms

| # | mechanism | side | how to test it alone |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | dirty-slot scan | SH-2 | count slots visited vs slots dirty |
| 2 | slot → tile code (`md_tag`) | SH-2 | dump tags, compare to the name table |
